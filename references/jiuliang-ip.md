# 卡通九两 IP

## Role

卡通九两 is the personal-IP actor for 半斤九两 visual scenes. He is not a decoration. In every scene he should be doing the core action that explains the article's point.

## Visual DNA

- Q-version young AI mentor / one-person-business partner.
- Silver-blue-purple fluffy hair with clean edges.
- Round glasses and bright eyes.
- Orange inner shirt.
- White or light-gray tech jacket.
- Black pants.
- Black, white, and orange sneakers.
- Optional small dark backpack when it helps silhouette continuity.
- Warm, clever, energetic, and trustworthy.
- Cute enough to be approachable, not so cute that it becomes childish.

## Action Vocabulary

Prefer actions that make the article's abstract point physical:

- holding, pulling, pushing, shielding, sorting, filtering, stamping, pinning, carrying, checking, dragging, pointing, opening, catching, untangling, measuring, presenting, typing, thinking, celebrating.

For article images, choose one action. Do not make Jiuliang do multiple unrelated things in the same image.

## Guardrails

- Do not use Xiaohei, black bean characters, black capsule mascots, generic robots, generic anime boys, or animal mascots.
- Do not change Jiuliang into a realistic human portrait.
- Do not over-style hair into neon cyberpunk or fantasy hair.
- Do not remove the glasses unless the user explicitly asks.
- Do not replace the orange/white/black clothing structure with random outfits.
- Do not make the IP too young, toy-like, or kindergarten-like.
- Do not make the scene depend on reading a long title. The action should carry the meaning.

## Recognition Test

Before delivering, ask:

```text
If I remove the article title, can a repeat viewer still recognize this as 半斤九两 / 汤九两's cartoon IP?
```

If the answer is no, regenerate or edit the character.
